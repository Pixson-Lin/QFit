QFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-09
Last updated: 2026-10-09
Contact: pixson.srv@gmail.com

In short: No sign-in. On-device permissions only. Nothing uploaded to the developer. QFit runs on your phone, writes simulated activity into Health Connect on this device, and keeps a local history in the app. There is no QFit server and no developer-operated database.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the QFit application (“QFit”, “the app”, “we”) handles information when you use it. QFit may be installed via sideload or Google Play (including testing tracks).

2. What QFit does

QFit is a step simulator. You choose a type and duration; the app generates simulated walking/running activity and writes steps, distance, and exercise sessions into Health Connect on the same device. Other apps you authorize may read that data under their own policies. QFit does not use Google Sign-In and does not use the Google Fit / Fitness API.

3. Information the app processes

3.1 Health Connect (on your device)

  • QFit requests permission to write steps, distance, and exercise sessions.
  • These records are created by the app and stored in Health Connect on your device. This is health and fitness data.
  • QFit does not request permission to read your broader Health Connect history for analytics, advertising, or profiling.
  • QFit does not upload Health Connect records to the developer or to any server we operate.

3.2 Local app data (on your device)

  • Run history and segment records (start/end times, generated steps/distance, write status) in a local on-device database (Room).
  • App preferences such as type, duration, and environment toggles (for example Run notification) in local app storage.
  • You can clear local history in the app. Uninstalling QFit removes local app data. Clearing history or uninstalling does not automatically delete records already stored in Health Connect.

3.3 Device permissions used to run the feature

To keep writes reliable while a run is active, QFit may use Android permissions such as notifications (optional Run notification), foreground service, battery optimization exemption, exact alarms, and wake locks. These control how the app runs on your device; they are not used to collect account data or to send personal information to the developer.

3.4 What we do not collect

  • No Google account / no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No internet permission for uploading your data to us (the app does not send your data to a QFit backend).

4. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Generate and write the activity records you request into Health Connect.
  • Show run progress and local history inside the app.
  • Keep the run process alive according to the permissions you grant.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. On your device, QFit interacts with Health Connect (and Android system settings you open) only as needed for the features above. Other apps can read Health Connect data only if you grant them access.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local QFit history: kept on device until you clear history in the app or uninstall QFit.
  • Health Connect records: managed by Health Connect / Android. Revoke QFit’s Health Connect permissions or delete records in the Health Connect app to remove data written there.

7. Security

QFit relies on Android’s app sandbox and Health Connect permission controls. Because there is no QFit account or QFit cloud sync, there is no developer-side account database to secure.

8. Children’s privacy

QFit is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: pixson.srv@gmail.com.
